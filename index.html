Privacy Policy

Time Tracker

Last updated: August 17, 2026

Overview

Time Tracker is a time-tracking application that stores your time-tracking information locally on your device. The developer does not operate a server that collects or stores your time-tracking data.

Information stored on your device

The application may store information such as tasks, time entries, and application settings locally on your device. This information is not transmitted to the developer's servers.

Google Calendar integration

Time Tracker provides an optional Google Calendar integration. If you choose to configure and use this feature, the application uses the Google Calendar API to create calendar events in the calendar you specify.

Information required to create these events, such as task names and start and end times, is transmitted directly to Google through the Google Calendar API. The developer does not receive or store this information on its own servers.

The Google Calendar integration is optional. You can use the application without configuring it.

Google Calendar credentials

If you configure Google Calendar synchronization using service account credentials, those credentials are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to the developer's servers.

You are responsible for managing the Google Cloud project, service account, credentials, and calendar permissions that you configure for this feature.

Data sharing

Time Tracker does not sell your personal information and does not share your information with the developer's own third-party services.

When you use the Google Calendar integration, information is sent to Google as necessary to create the requested calendar events. Google's handling of that information is governed by Google's own privacy policies and terms.

Data deletion

You can delete locally stored application data through the application's available controls or by uninstalling the application. Calendar events created by Time Tracker are stored in your Google Calendar and may need to be deleted separately from Google Calendar.

Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, you can contact the developer through the contact information provided on the Google Play Store listing.
